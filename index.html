Capstone: Sync with an Investor

From: Mark Sterling (Founder, Sterling Growth Ops)
Subject: Sync with partner next week

“Hey VA! Need to set up a 45-minute sync with my investor, Sarah Lin (sarah@linventures.com), for next Thursday. Let's aim for 3:00 PM my time (US Eastern Time). Check my 'Client Work' calendar—I have an internal team standup until 2:30 PM, so make sure there's enough breathing room. We use Zoom (use my room: zoom.us/j/99887766), so please DO NOT use Google Meet. Add a quick 3-bullet agenda: 1) Q3 Performance Review, 2) Growth Projections, 3) Seed Extension Terms. Don't let guests edit the event. Thanks!”

Resolve the traps

Buffer

The standup ends at 2:30 PM EST. A 3:00 PM start leaves a 30-minute buffer.

Duration

Make it 45 minutes, ending at 3:45 PM EST.

Calendar

Use the Client Work calendar, not a personal calendar.

Conference

Use the supplied Zoom room. If Google Meet appears after guests are added, remove it.

Guests

Add Sarah Lin and do not allow guests to modify the event. Review the guest-permission options before saving.

Timezone + date

Verify both the client's day/time and your Philippine day/time. The local date may be the following day depending on the client's DST status.

How to Execute This Task: READ → DECIDE → DO → CHECK
READ: Find the meeting time, attendee email, platform preference, calendar, duration, and buffer needs in the request.
DECIDE: Which calendar belongs to this client? What buffer is needed? Does the date/time cross midnight in the Philippines?
DO: Create the event, set the 45-minute duration, add Sarah, use the Zoom room, add the three agenda points, and adjust guest permissions.
CHECK: Reopen the saved event. Did Google Calendar inject an unwanted Google Meet link? Is it on Client Work? Does the date match the client's day as well as your local day? Are guest permissions correct?
